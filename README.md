LAWDIATOR: The Legal Arena of GenAI Entertainment
🧠 “Where Law Meets Logic, Legends & Laughter.”

Lawdiator is a creative web application that combines Generative AI, legal understanding, and entertainment into a unique digital experience. It’s not a dry law tool — it’s a playful, insightful, and interactive space for everyone to explore legal concepts through fun, relatable, and engaging formats.

🎯 Purpose:
To make law more understandable, fun, and accessible to the everyday user using GenAI — by turning boring documents, complex legal jargon, and even courtroom scenarios into something users will enjoy interacting with.

🔥 THE 4 CORE MODULES (FEATURES):
1️⃣  What If Generator – “Legally Rewritten Movie Scene”
🔍 Function: Reimagines famous movie scenes under Indian legal logic.

2️⃣ Legal Tales – AI Short Generator
🔍 Function: Transforms real Indian legal cases into dramatized scripts or animated storytelling.

3️⃣  Legal Escape Room – Puzzle Game
🔍 Function: A legal puzzle game where users must find loopholes, legal arguments, or technicalities to escape bizarre legal situations.

4️⃣  PolicyPal – The 2-Line Truth Bot
🔍 Function: Upload any policy, privacy document, or contract → AI gives a 2-line summary + flags risk + answers your questions.
